3 · The knob pile in Settings

Today there are five controls: Obscurity (how many of your own plays hide an artist), Popularity curve, Underground (hard cap: popularity ≤ 50), Deep discovery (a second similarity hop) and Adventurous. Really there are two axes: how famous and how far from your taste. Each option below says what it removes.

A “How famous?” slider merges the curve and Underground: its bottom stop is the hard cap. The Wander toggle absorbs Deep discovery. The play threshold becomes a fixed default.
